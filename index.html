( 02 )About Kamra, Pakistan

The person behind
the sound.

I'm Hasaan Kiani — an 18-year-old independent music creator from Kamra, Pakistan. No label, no team, no formula. Just a young artist building a sound of his own, one honest record at a time.

I write my own words, shape my own melodies and produce my own records. Every idea starts small — a voice memo, a line in a notebook — and grows at its own pace, through late nights, trial, error and constant experimentation.

Independence isn't a limitation to me; it's the whole point. It means every sound you hear from me is genuinely mine — learned, made and finished by hand.

"I'd rather release one record that's completely true to me than a hundred that aren't."
